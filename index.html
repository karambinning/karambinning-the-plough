№ I · Welcome

A charming village pub on the edge of Hemel Hempstead, serving a full à la carte alongside a gold-standard carvery.

Beer-battered fish & chips. British beef burgers, ground in-house. Homemade pies from the kitchen, dressed with proper gravy. Our menu sticks to classics done well, with ingredients sourced from family farms and butchers we know by name.

There is room outside for long Sundays in the sun and room inside for birthdays, christenings, baby showers and quieter celebrations of life. We do not over-think it — fresh food, generous portions, and a warm welcome at the door.

Whether you are calling in for a Tuesday pie or settling in for a Saturday with friends, we are pleased you found us.

№ II · The Carvery

A proper carvery, seven days a week.

Crisp roasties, giant homemade Yorkshires, all the trimmings — and gravy poured generously.

Our carvery is what we are best known for. The meats are cooked through the morning and carved to order; the sides are made fresh and topped up all day. Sundays bring out the full table — Mondays through Saturdays we keep the same standards, only quieter.

№ III · The Week, In Brief

A few standing arrangements, weekly.

Every Tuesday

Pie Tuesday

One of our homemade pies, your choice of chips or creamy mash, paired with a house lager, a glass of house wine, or a soft drink. The right way to spend a Tuesday.

Every Saturday

Summer Sessions

Live entertainment, food and drinks running through the weekend. Gather a few friends — there is no better way to round off a Saturday at The Plough.

Sunday Carvery

Sundays done the right way. A proper British carvery: perfectly cooked meats carved just right, with all the trimmings, sides and gravy you know and love.
